Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Dayframe does not collect, store or share any personal information.

No accounts

You never sign in or give us your name or email.

No analytics or tracking

The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Your puzzle stays on your device

Your progress, timer and settings are saved only on your iPhone. The Home Screen widget reads the same on-device data.

Purchases

Apple handles the optional Dayframe Unlimited purchase through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers those transactions.

If you delete the app, everything it stored is deleted with it.
