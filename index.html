Dayframe

Support

Dayframe is a daily calendar puzzle. Fit the wooden pieces onto the board so that every square is covered except today's month and date. In Weekday mode, leave the day of the week uncovered too.

How to play

Place a piece

Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. It snaps to the nearest spot that fits.

Turn or flip

Tap a piece to select it, then tap it again to turn it. Use Turn and Flip for more control.

Take it back

Drag a piece off the board, or select it and tap Remove.

Questions

Does every date have a solution?

Yes. All 366 dates in Classic, including February 29, can be solved, and so can every date and weekday combination in Weekday.

What's Weekday?

A harder puzzle that adds the days of the week and its own set of ten pieces. Leave the month, the date and the day of the week uncovered. Switch modes at the top of the screen.

What's free?

Classic every day, Weekday every Sunday, and one hint a day. Dayframe Unlimited, a one-time purchase, unlocks Weekday every day, any date in both modes, and unlimited hints.

I bought Dayframe Unlimited on another device

Tap the date at the top of the screen, then tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing is supported.

I have a code

Tap the date at the top of the screen, then tap Redeem Code.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Dayframe. It shows your board just as you left it.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. The timer pauses when you leave the app.
